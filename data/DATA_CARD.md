# JanSetu Data Card

## 1. Dataset Overview
- **Name**: JanSetu Infrastructure Demand & Deficit Dataset (Synthetic)
- **Purpose**: To simulate citizen reporting and district-level infrastructure deficits across India to test the JanSetu priority scoring engine.

## 2. Privacy & Ethical Considerations
- **PII Scrubbing**: The AI extraction layer actively ignores names and phone numbers.
- **Fairness**: The system is designed to prevent "loudest voice wins" by weighting the district baseline deficit.
