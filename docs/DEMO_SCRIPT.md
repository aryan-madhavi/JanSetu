# JanSetu Demo Script

**0:00 - 0:15 | Intro & The Problem**
"Welcome to JanSetu. In India, public infrastructure demands are scattered across local offices and social media, making it impossible for policymakers to prioritize effectively. JanSetu changes that."

**0:15 - 0:45 | Multimodal Citizen Input**
"Let's see how a citizen interacts. They can use Telegram, WhatsApp, or our PWA. They upload a picture of a broken road and leave a voice note in Hindi: 'हमारे गाँव की सड़क टूट गई है'. JanSetu's Gemini 2.5 integration extracts the location, severity, and automatically translates the request, replying to the citizen in their local language via Text-to-Speech."

**0:45 - 1:15 | The Policymaker Dashboard**
"Now, let's look at the policymaker's view. Our dashboard aggregates thousands of these data points. Here, we see Priority Clusters—JanSetu compares citizen demand signals against actual district infrastructure deficit data to generate an actionable Priority Score."

**1:15 - 1:45 | Ask The Data**
"Using the 'Ask the Data' feature powered by Gemini 2.5 Pro, a policymaker can naturally ask, 'Which districts need water infrastructure the most?' and get instant, data-backed insights."

**1:45 - 2:00 | Conclusion**
"JanSetu aligns government spending with real citizen needs. Thank you."
